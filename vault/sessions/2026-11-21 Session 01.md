## Results (factual, with file paths)
- EXP-01 (`experiments/exp_01_mit3_language/RESULTS.md`): corpus right in 6 of 6 conversations; all 10 true
  copying heads found; one extra head (python 3.0) labelled copy in both Python conversations.
- EXP-04 (`experiments/exp_04_medqa_baseline/processed/`), letter-logit accuracy:

  | Split | n | Phi-3.5 | MediPhi | only Phi right | only MediPhi right | McNemar p |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | test | 1,273 | 0.5719 | 0.5632 | 57 | 46 | 0.324 |
  | dev | 1,272 | 0.5841 | 0.5951 | 39 | 53 | 0.175 |
  | train pool | 3,000 | 0.6037 | 0.5923 | 129 | 95 | 0.027 |

  `microsoft/MediPhi` is not better than Phi-3.5 on MedQA with this scoring. The MediPhi paper's own appendix
  (Table 13) reports MedQA 0.486 for Phi-3.5, 0.491 for MediPhi and 0.548 for MediPhi-Instruct.
- EXP-05a (`experiments/exp_05_medical_circuit/processed/weight_diff_summary.json`): the difference is small
  and spread out. Global relative norm 1.57 %; every attention and MLP matrix in all 32 layers differs, with
  50 to 65 % of entries changed and a broad spectrum (90 % of the energy needs about 1,500 to 2,400 of 3,072
  singular values), so it is not a low-rank adapter. Normalization weights are unchanged except in layers 0-2.
  Relative change of the attention output matrices falls with depth (4.9 % at layer 0 to 0.8 % at layer 31);
  query/key/value changes grow with depth (0.1 % to 1.5 %); MLP changes stay near 1.6 to 1.8 %.
  The repo ships `mergekit_config.yml`: `breadcrumbs_ties` over five experts (PubMed, Clinical, MedCode,
  MedWiki, Guidelines) with Phi-3.5 as base.

## Update 2026-11-22 evening - paper's MedQA numbers reproduced, new direction, a crash

### Findings
- The paper gives no MedQA protocol. Scored in the plain-text format of lm-evaluation-harness's `medqa_4options`
  task, our numbers match its Table 13: Phi-3.5 0.4855 (paper 0.486), MediPhi 0.4808 (0.491), MediPhi-Instruct

- Mechanism at the behaviour level: with the bare prompt Phi-3.5 puts 4 % of its next-token probability on the
  answer letters (it would rather start a sentence), MediPhi 10 %, MediPhi-Instruct 41 %.
- Details: `experiments/exp_04_medqa_baseline/RESULTS.md` (Protocol C), [[EXP-04 MedQA baseline - Phi-3.5 vs MediPhi]].

## Update 2026-11-23 12:00 - patching, looping scan and chat-template control done; the night was slow
